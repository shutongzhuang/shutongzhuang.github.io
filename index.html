On-Policy and Privileged Self-Distillation as a Policy-Improvement Loop

Research internship project Research manuscript · Unpublished August 2026

This survey develops a unified framework for understanding how delayed outcomes, verifier feedback, sibling rollouts, tool returns, and other privileged signals can be converted into dense learning targets. It keeps objectives, exact gradients, estimators, detached surrogates, and tensor implementations conceptually separate, and compares how external teachers and privileged self-teachers improve an on-policy student.

Physics Publications

Branes and Representations of DAHA C∨C₁: Affine Braid Group Action on Category

Junkang Huang, Satoshi Nawata, Yutai Zhang, and Shutong Zhuang · 2024

This work uses brane quantization to connect compactly supported Lagrangian A-branes with finite-dimensional representations of the spherical double affine Hecke algebra. It reveals an affine D₄ braid-group action and provides geometric insight into the low-energy dynamics of SU(2) Nf=4 Seiberg–Witten theory.

Central Charges and Vacuum Moduli of 2d N=(0,4) Theories from Class S

Wei Cui, Junkang Huang, Zi-Xiao Huang, Satoshi Nawata, and Shutong Zhuang · 2025

This work investigates two-dimensional N=(0,4) supersymmetric theories obtained from topologically twisted reductions of four-dimensional N=2 class S theories. It proposes central-charge formulas and studies special and twisted Higgs branches through Hilbert-series calculations for SU(2) theories.
