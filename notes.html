01 Reinforcement learning English

From Value Estimation to Policy Optimization

A First-Principles Review of Reinforcement Learning and Language-Model Post-Training

A connected derivation from Bellman recursion and policy gradients to language-model post-training and supervision on student-generated prefixes.

Value estimation Policy gradients PPO & GRPO On-policy distillation

8 sections · Appendix A · References

Read PDF ↗ 28 pages · Updated 10 Sep 2026
Overview & full contents

Overview

The review begins with the learning problem, value estimation, and the policy-gradient identity. It develops baselines, critics, GAE, importance sampling, and finite policy updates, then examines TRPO, PPO, deterministic and soft actor–critic methods, and distributed learning.

The second half translates these ideas to language models: preference and reward learning, reference regularization, DPO, GRPO and RLOO, supervised fine-tuning, and on-policy distillation. Objectives, exact gradients, sampled estimators, detached surrogates, and implemented losses are tracked separately. Appendix A gives derivations and boundary conditions for performance differences, GAE, KL estimation, sequence divergence, and potential shaping.

02 Deep learning theory English

The Principles of Deep Learning Theory

A Derivation-Centred Companion · Based on Roberts & Yaida

A chapter-by-chapter companion connecting random initialization, criticality, finite-width corrections, Bayesian conditioning, and neural tangent kernel dynamics.

Gaussian limits Finite width Criticality NTK dynamics

Chapters 0–11 and ∞ · Appendices A–B · References

Read PDF ↗ 28 pages · Updated 10 Sep 2026
Overview & full contents

Overview

These notes follow The Principles of Deep Learning Theory by Daniel A. Roberts and Sho Yaida, with research in collaboration with Boris Hanin. They retain the calculational path from Gaussian expectations and deep linear networks to preactivation flow, critical initialization, and finite-width fluctuations.

The learning chapters develop Bayesian conditioning, gradient dynamics, the flow and criticality of the NTK, frozen-kernel learning, representation learning, and the dynamical corrections at the end of training. The appendices cover information and residual networks. Reusable identities replace repetitive component expansions, with the assumptions and limits of the approximations stated alongside the derivations.
